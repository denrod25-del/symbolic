# 🗄️ Archive

Cold storage for anything from **Projects**, **Areas**, or **Resources** that's no longer active — completed projects, areas you've stepped away from, topics you've cooled on.

## Why archive instead of delete
- Keeps your active folders uncluttered and your attention on *now*.
- Nothing is lost — finished work stays searchable and can be revived by moving it back.

> [!tip] Weekly review habit
> When you finish a project, don't leave it lingering in `01 Projects`. Move it here. A clean active workspace is the reward.
