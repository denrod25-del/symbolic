# 🏠 Home

Welcome to your **second brain** — a place to *capture* what resonates, *organize* it so it's actionable, *distill* it to its essence, and *express* new ideas from it (the [CODE](https://fortelabs.com/blog/the-code-method/) method).

This note is your dashboard. Pin it: open it, then **right-click the tab → Pin**.

## 🗂️ The PARA system

Everything lives in one of four folders, ordered by how soon you'll act on it:

| Folder | What goes here | Time horizon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Projects MOC\|01 Projects]] | Goals with a deadline and an end | Now |
| [[Areas MOC\|02 Areas]] | Responsibilities to maintain over time | Ongoing |
| [[Resources MOC\|03 Resources]] | Topics & reference material of interest | Someday |
| `04 Archive` | Finished or inactive items | Cold storage |

Plus two support folders: **`00 Inbox`** (capture everything here first, sort later) and **`05 Templates`**.

## 🌊 Daily flow
- **Capture** → drop anything into `00 Inbox` (or today's [[Daily]] note)
- **Clarify** → once a day, empty the Inbox: move each item to P / A / R, or delete it
- **Connect** → link new notes to at least one existing note
- **Review** → weekly, scan Projects & Areas; archive what's done

> [!tip] Start here
> 1. Open the **Templates** core plugin and try *Insert template → Daily Note*.
> 2. Capture three things into `00 Inbox` today.
> 3. Tomorrow, sort them. That's the whole habit.
