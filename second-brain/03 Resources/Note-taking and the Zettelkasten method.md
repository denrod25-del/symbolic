# Note-taking and the Zettelkasten method

> [!info] A reference note on the ideas this vault is built around.

## 💡 Summary
**Zettelkasten** ("slip-box") is a method for thinking *in* notes rather than just storing them. You write small, atomic notes — one idea each — in your own words, and link them densely. Insight emerges from the connections, not from any single note.

## 🔑 Key points
- **Atomic:** one idea per note, so it can be reused in many contexts.
- **In your own words:** writing forces understanding; copying doesn't.
- **Linked:** a note's value comes from what it connects to. Always link to at least one other note.
- **Bottom-up:** structure ([[Map of Content|MOCs]]) emerges from the notes; you don't impose it up front.
- Pairs well with **PARA** (organize by actionability) and **progressive summarization** (distill on revisit).
