# One-Page Positioning Brief — Palm Beach County Plumbing Startup

## The bet

Be **the water-intelligence + communication plumbing company** in Palm Beach County. Competitors
compete on plumbing; almost none compete on water science, homeowner education, or transparency.
That is the gap.

## Why it works (3 facts)

1. **The water is genuinely problematic — and it's documented.** Very hard (~19 gpg in PBCWUD),
   disinfected with **chloramine** (cheap big-box filters fail on it), and several systems carry
   **PFOS/PFOA above the EPA's new 4-ppt limit** with no mandated utility fix until **2031**.
2. **The #1 customer complaint is communication, not price** (~38% vs ~21% in national survey
   data). Locally, nobody owns text updates, arrival tracking, or photo/video summaries.
3. **Nobody publishes intelligence.** No surveyed competitor offers a water-quality report,
   educational content, video, or a customer portal. Financing and memberships, by contrast, are
   everywhere.

## Positioning statement

> For Palm Beach County homeowners who want to know — not guess — what's in their water and what
> their plumbing actually needs, [Brand] is the plumber that delivers address-specific water
> intelligence, a tracked Plumbing Health Score, and radically transparent communication. Unlike
> high-volume and franchise plumbers who sell repairs, we sell understanding.

## Four pillars

| Pillar | What it is | Status of the gap |
|---|---|---|
| Water intelligence | Free address-specific report: hardness + chloramine + PFAS by utility | Lead magnet (commoditized as a product — use for acquisition) |
| Communication | Text updates, live ETA tracking, photo/video job summaries, customer portal | **Open locally — primary differentiator** |
| Plumbing Health Score | Standardized annual scored inspection, tracked year over year | **Genuine white space** |
| Integrated layer | Install + monitor Moen/Phyn devices; auto-populate service records | **White space (the bundle no one offers)** |

## What to copy vs. what to invent

- **Copy (table stakes):** financing (GreenSky/Synchrony/Wisetack), a membership plan (~$15/mo
  or ~$99/yr), online booking, on-time guarantee.
- **Invent (moat):** the water report, the Health Score, transparent comms, the integrated
  device+records bundle.
- **Do NOT try to out-build:** EWG (free water reports), Moen/Phyn (leak hardware), Centriq-style
  home-records apps (a graveyard — Centriq shut down Jan 2026). Wrap these, don't rebuild them.

## Primary competitors to watch

- **Cleary Plumbing & Air** — closest to this model (membership + financing + modern ops). Most
  likely to copy. Beat them on the intelligence layer they lack.
- **All Star Plumbing & Air** — strongest reputation (4.7★/1,000+). Out-educate, don't
  out-advertise.
- **The Plumbing Experts** — largest fleet but only 3.3–3.8★. Their unhappy customers are the
  acquisition funnel.

## Messaging guardrails

Say "above EPA's **new** health-based limits" or "above EWG health guidelines." Never "illegal"
or "unsafe" — all county utilities currently meet enforceable federal standards, and PFAS
exceedances are against not-yet-enforced limits. Stay accurate to stay credible (and liable-free).
